# UI quality bar

## 1. The bar
- Owner's rule (2026-10-18; PROTOKOL's quality bar): every shipped screen looks finished: cute characters and objects, soft light, one harmonious palette, soft motion, GUI Pro UI, Synty or our own models. Grey boxes, flat placeholder shapes and bare Unity widgets never reach a store build.
- Order: style guide (`docs/ai/tasarim/gorsel-stil.md`) and one finished-looking screen before content multiplies.
- Done (pre-release test stage) = the test list green on the 5-profile matrix in both languages AND the honest side-by-side (§5) as §5 rules.
- Revenue screens first. In hybrid-casual puzzles the lose/continue popup earns the most: "fail offer" packs sit in the top rows of the Color Block Jam, Bus Traffic Fever!, Knit Out and Point Out IAP lists, and they are Knit Out's main income. Polish order: lose/continue → win → home → HUD → shop → the rest.
- References by genre (study, never copy; rakip-oyun-incelemesi.md): match-3 Royal Match, Royal Kingdom · block Block Blast · jam Color Block Jam · sort Magic Sort · yarn Knit Out · screw Screwdom · hole All in Hole · arrow Arrows – Puzzle Escape · mahjong Vita Mahjong · tower defense Kingdom Rush, Bloons TD 6 · survivor Survivor.io, Archero 2 · 4X Whiteout Survival, Last War. Refresh the list every 6 months (AppMagic, Sensor Tower).

## 2. What the leaders do (measured 2026-10-18; phone portrait unless noted)
- **One dominant action.** Royal Match home: "Level N" (green) and "Tasks" (orange), each 41-42% of the width and 11% of the height. Vita Mahjong and Block Blast: one play button. On tablets buttons keep their physical size and the group is centred (Royal Match: 66% of the width), never stretched.
- **Thin edges, big middle.** Top-bar pills 4-5% of the screen height; an action game's whole HUD ≤ 6% of the screen; boards 86-90% of the width (Royal Match 86%, Block Blast 89%).
- **Few HUD items.** Block Blast: best score, score, settings. Vita: back, level, menu; no timer, score or move counter.
- **Helpers:** round, 64-65 dp (15-16% of the width), icon only, red count badge, "+" at zero, padlock and "Lv N" while locked (Vita; Royal Match boosters 15.5%).
- **Three-layer buttons:** saturated body, light top gloss, dark bottom lip, a 2-4 px dark outline, cream or white label with a dark outline. Royal Match green #85CF02 / gloss #AEDF56 / lip #267C01; Whiteout blue #4fa5fc / #9fe7ff / #0247a2. Nothing flat.
- **Contrast hierarchy:** pieces > board > background > HUD (Block Blast: saturated bevelled pieces, board #252a52, background gradient #4557ad → #546dbe).
- **Rewards travel:** every reward flies from where it was earned into its counter, which pulses (Royal Match). Praise escalates ("Good!" → "Excellent!" → "Amazing!") with "+N" score flights and a light strip across the board for combos (Block Blast).
- **Characters react:** Royal Match's king tenses as moves run out.
- **Readable for older players:** Vita's tiles ≥ 56 dp (13.7% of the width), ≥ 36 dp on the densest board, symbol contrast ≥ 5.9:1, three distinct tile states (free, blocked grey, selected with the whole face tinted).
- **Choice cards** (survivor-likes): 3 cards, each 27-31% of the width and ~34% of the height, over a dark dimmer under a ribbon title; the rarity colour repeats in the title, the frame and a light column (blue rare, purple epic, gold legendary).
- **Dark themes stay friendly:** deep navy or dark brown grounds (#224268, #1d1916), never black; a saturated primary; off-white icon plates.
- **Result screens** (gameplay recordings of Royal Match, Color Block Jam (CBJ), Block Blast, Candy Crush, Vita Mahjong, frame by frame):
  - The panel arrives with its dimmer in one frame (Royal Match, Block Blast; CBJ's dimmer 0.13 s); the motion lives in the content: the star settles from 1.5-2× in 0.2 s, the title grows or types in 0.15-0.35 s with confetti, the panel is still by 0.55-1.15 s. No leader grows a result panel slowly. Dimmers are 78-82% black.
  - Hybrid-casual shows the panel 0.2-0.6 s after the last move (CBJ, Vita); match-3 runs longer bonus chains (Candy Crush ~5 s, Royal Match ~9 s), every part skippable with a visible "Tap to Skip".
  - Rewards always flow into their counters (CBJ ~0.8 s, Royal Match ~3.3 s); scores count up in 0.95-2.7 s, and Block Blast's play button grows in (0.2 s) the moment the count ends.
  - One primary, 52-64% of the width and 6-9% of the height (≈ 53-75 dp); 39% each when two share a row. CBJ's rewarded "▶ x2 Coin 80" is green, big and on top; the plain "Continue 40" is smaller but visible at the same time; no "No thanks", no hidden way out.
  - Losing has two poles: Royal Match's sales screen (Play On for 900 coins, a 60%-off card, one rotating "what you'll lose" card, all red) and Block Blast's no-offer screen ("Not This Time" / "You Can Do It!", the remaining goals, the streak lost, Retry 0.66 s later).
  - Praise varies and is personal: Vita rotates its win title ("Brilliant!", "Fearless!", "Finally!") with a line about how you played, and its next button shows the level and its difficulty colour; a new record gets its own celebration (crown or trophy, confetti, its own colour) in the same layout. Colour carries the mood: wins warm blue and gold, losses red.

## 3. Screen recipes (job · layout · must-haves · feel; timings in oyun-hissi.md)
- **Home / map.** Job: one tap to the next level. Primary "Level 12" / "Bölüm 12" (not just "Play") in the bottom third, ≥ 64 dp tall, ≥ 50% of the width alone or ≥ 40% each when a second equal button carries the meta (tasks). Top bar: currency pills (icon overlapping the pill's left end, a "+" badge opens the shop) and settings. At most 4 side buttons, each icon + short label, badged only when something waits. A bottom nav has ≤ 5 tabs, ~10% of the height, and only the active tab is labelled. The scene is alive (slow idle motion), never a flat colour.
- **HUD.** Job: goal and danger at a glance; the board owns the middle (86-90% of the width). Puzzle HUD ≤ 4 items: level or goal, moves or timer as the biggest number, settings. Helpers at the bottom: round ≥ 64 dp, count badge, "+" at zero (opens the rewarded or coin offer), padlock and "Lv N". Action games: pills 4-5% of the height, numbers in meaning colours (lives red or orange, gold yellow), pause art ≥ 36 pt on a ≥ 48 dp hit area, action buttons ~11-12% of the height. A changing number pulses (scale 1.15 → 1, 0.15 s).
- **Level start** (games with boosters): goal, up to 3 boosters to toggle, one Play.
- **Win.** Job: reward and move on. The panel within 0.2-0.6 s of the last move; a longer bonus chain (leftover moves into rewards) ≤ ~3 s or skippable with a visible "Tap to skip / Geçmek için dokun". The panel and its dimmer (~0.78) arrive at once or within 0.15 s; the content moves: star or trophy settles from 1.5-2× in 0.2 s, ribbon title 0.15-0.35 s with confetti, sound and a success haptic; still by 1.2 s. Stars or a progress fill arrive one by one; coins count up and fly to the top-bar counter (0.8-3 s), which pulses. Praise rotates (4-5 titles) with a line about how they played (no mistakes, first try, hard level); a record gets its own celebration in the same layout. One primary, 52-64% of the width ("Next", or "Level 13" with its difficulty colour), tappable ≤ 1.2 s after the panel opens or the moment a count ends (growing in, 0.2 s). A rewarded ×2 is a separate button with the video icon AND the doubled amount ("+20", not "Double"); the plain claim is visible from the start (no delayed, tiny "No thanks"). A tap skips the sequence.
- **Lose and continue** (the revenue screen). Job: say what happened, show how close it was, offer one fair way on, make retry one tap (adil-zorluk-ve-basarisizlik.md). The title encourages, never blames ("So close!" / "Az kaldı!"; an icon in no-text games); the remaining goals or progress ("2 left!", a bar). Primary: continue with the exact cost inside the button with its icon (+5 moves for 900 coins, or video icon + "+1"); close × visible from the first frame. A second continue may cost more but adds a bonus (Royal Match adds a rocket). At most one "what you'll lose" card (streak, hearts), and only the truth. An optional purchase offer (price: the manager) sits below the continue as a second card, never in its place. Secondary: retry, ≤ 0.7 s after the title.
- **Choice cards** (level-up, boosters): 3 cards at 27-31% of the width each, the game paused under a dimmer, ribbon title, rarity colour three times, "NEW!" tag, a slot bar showing the build; cards enter staggered; reroll is a secondary with its cost shown.
- **Shop.** Job: honest value at a glance. Cards with icon art, the amount as the biggest text, the price from the store (localized string with currency, never typed in a prefab), at most one "Best value" and one "Most popular" tag. Remove Ads says what it removes (often a bundle with coins). Restore Purchases on iOS. Purchases fly into the counters.
- **Rewarded offer** (odullu-reklam.md): opt-in button with a video icon (clapperboard or play, not a monitor) and the reward as a number; declining keeps the game going; one colour for rewarded across the game. No ad ready: disabled with a small spinner, hidden after ~5 s, and the next option becomes the primary.
- **Settings.** Job: control and trust. Rows: sound, music, haptics (separate), language, reduce motion, privacy choices, privacy policy, licences, restore purchases (iOS), support, version. Rows ≥ 56 dp, grouped, switches with on/off text, not colour alone. No login, rate, push or social rows unless the game really has them.
- **Popups.** One at a time from a queue; none over the first session's first level; at most one at app start (daily reward OR offer). Width 86-92% of the portrait width, title ribbon, close × at the top right with a ≥ 48 dp hit area, dimmer 70-82% black (leaders ~78%; judge it on a screenshot: linear colour space lightens it), content padding ≥ 24 dp, empty area ≤ 40% of the panel.
- **First launch and tutorial** (ilk-dakika.md): playable in ≤ 10 s; level 1 is the tutorial; spotlight (dim + hole) and a hand that taps with a ring pulse; one line of ≤ 7 words at a time; first win in 30-60 s; no permission or offer before the first win.
- **Loading.** Never blank: logo or scene art and a progress bar; hide hitches behind a fade ≤ 0.3 s.

## 4. Don'ts
- No grey boxes, default Unity buttons, LiberationSans fallback glyphs, "New Text", lorem or debug text in a store build.
- No two primaries on one screen; no red for "Later" or "Close"; no colour-only meaning; no hidden or delayed decline.
- No text baked into images (both languages); no hard-coded prices or currency signs.
- No popup chains at start, no offer before the first win, no ad between a tap and its result (Google Play also bans interstitials at level start, before the splash and on exit; rewarded ads only with the player's explicit opt-in).
- Never copy another game's art, layout, icons or text one to one; reference images stay out of the game and store art (under `Logs/`; the pre-research's evidence in `docs/ai/tasarim/on-arastirma/`: on-arastirma.md).

## 5. Honest check (quality bar, last step)
- Scored screens: home, HUD, win, lose (continue) and shop, in the pre-release test stage; a plan that changes one of them also scores it at its closure (BAKIM.md's honest check). For each: a sheet with our screen beside the same screen of two references (store screenshots or preview-video frames, review only, kept under `Logs/`, never in the game, store page or videos). Score 1-5: hierarchy clear in 1 s, readability, reward feel, polish (depth, light, motion), consistency with our other screens. **Floor 3** (the one place the floor is set; others point here): below 3 on any criterion blocks the release → fix, then send. **Target 4** on win, lose and shop: a miss doesn't block, its reason goes in the report. Write the before and after scores in the closure report.
- Worked example (2026-10-18, a studio arrow puzzle's lose popup): measured problems (card 1.10:1 on the dimmed scene, two equal buttons, a monitor icon for video, a pink heart on gold at 1.86:1, no "how close", an empty upper half), fixed with the game's own GUI Pro parts: one gold primary (clapperboard, outlined heart, "+1"), a round retry button, a "how close" bar, the card ≥ 1.3:1 lighter than the dimmed scene.
