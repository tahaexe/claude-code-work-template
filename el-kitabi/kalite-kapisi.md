# Polish & Quality Gate

## Where it sits
- A fixed step in every new game's release plan and every big update (new levels, modes, meta, look, UI or store art; anything the rubric scores), after content-complete and the store-asset drafts, before the comprehensive test stage (BAKIM.md): content complete → gate `KAPI: GEÇTİ` → comprehensive test → release. Its fixes land before the test stage, so that stage tests the build that ships.
- No store or portal submission without `KAPI: GEÇTİ` for that version, resubmissions after a quality or 4.3 rejection included. A fix-only update (bugs, compliance, store policy) keeps the last pass.
- Pre-research (`on-arastirma.md`) sets the bar before the first plan; the gate proves the finished game meets it beside its rivals; the test stage proves the shipping build works. Feel, first minute, screen scores and the expert review stay in their entries; the gate scores their result.
- Why: portals judge the first seconds, the feel and originality and refuse clones, templates and asset flips [C1-C2, P1]; Apple's 4.3 rejects apps that can't be told apart from what is widely available and names shared code or assets, one template repackaged and purchased templates [A1-A3]. The template had the pieces but no stage that puts the finished game beside its top 3 and stops the release.

## Concept pre-check (concept time, `proje-kontrol-listesi.md` 1.4)
- The hook sentence: "<genre> where <the one thing> so that <feeling>" (Turkish: "<tür>, ama <tek fark>, böylece <his>").
- The top-3 table (the three most-played games of the exact subgenre): parity (what all three do) · our edge (≥1 concrete item none of them has, ideally 2, from mechanic, theme or world, audience, or meta) · our gaps (each fixed or accepted). Both open the dossier `docs/ai/tasarim/farklilik.md` (`sablonlar/farklilik-dosyasi.md`).
- Parity is not a hook: claims every rival makes (no timer, many levels, hints) set no game apart. No edge → change the mechanic, the world or the audience; a recolour, rename, refactor or obfuscation changes neither concept nor look [A4]. The hook needs depth behind it: a core loop that opens up over time [P1, E2].

## Entry
- Content complete (levels, meta, monetization, localization), no open crash, a style guide (`tasarim/gorsel-stil.md`), the store-asset drafts.
- The top 3 chosen (store charts and installs on the gate day, dated; normally the pre-research's leaders) and captured.

## Capture rules
- Same device, same moments, same length for all four games: a cold start from the icon tap; the first 60 s; the same actions (e.g. the first three moves, a win, a loss); the home, HUD, win, lose, shop and settings screens; the icon in the genre grid; store screenshots 1-3; the preview's first 5 s.
- Ours: Unity Recorder (`store-listing-optimization.md` › Recording in Unity) or `adb shell screenrecord` (no audio, ≤180 s a file); the rivals on the emulator or a phone, as the pre-research plays them (`on-arastirma.md` › Methods: never buy, never tap an ad on purpose). Audio is scored only from recordings with sound (`ebur128` loudness), else "not checked".
- **A rival with no device round** (it won't run on the emulator; no web build or web rival to play in a browser) [2026-10-23]: its icon, screenshots 1-3 and the preview's first 5 s from its store page; its moments (the first 60 s, the first three moves, a win, a loss, the home, HUD, shop and settings screens) from its most-watched gameplay video that starts at level 1; ours from editor captures under the same file names. Its device-only measurements → "not measured", with the store's size and rating instead; `olcumler.md` names each source and the reviewers get one line on it.
- Evidence in `docs/ai/tasarim/kalite-kapisi/<version>/`: `kor/` (the blind set: folders A-D with the same file names, `olcumler.md` with every measurement for all four, `ozellikler.md` with mechanic, theme or world, audience, meta and monetization written alike for all four; nothing names a game, logos and titles masked where cheap); `anahtar.md` (which letter is which, never shown to reviewers); the reviewers' and the fresh player's sheets; `puan.md`; the dossier as of the gate; the portfolio outputs. Clips ≤~15 s at 720p, captures ≤~1,280 px; long recordings stay in `Logs/` and are named.

## Checklist (target · how to measure)
Casual-puzzle defaults; the real bar is the top 3's median, so a genre may justify other numbers in the score sheet, with the rivals' measurement beside them.
- **First 60 s:** mobile cold start to the first playable input ≤5 s on the low-end target, a progress cue past 2 s · launch time and a log line at the first interactive frame. Web: load to `gameplayStart` ≤10 s on throttled 4G, ≤1 click · a scripted headless Chrome run. Tutorial by doing: ≤1 short line a step, ≤3 new things at once, no fail state in levels 1-2 (`ilk-dakika.md`). First win ≤60 s; the hook visible within 60 s, by level 3 at the latest · timestamps in the cold-start video. Before the first win only what law requires: no ad, rating prompt, offer, sign-in or optional permission; tutorial and level-funnel events fire (DebugView) [F1].
- **Core feel:** response in the touch frame within `oyun-hissi.md` §1's budgets · input-to-frame timestamps in the engine; the owner's 240 fps camera check once per game. A feedback matrix (select, valid and invalid move, clear or combo, near-miss, win, lose, reward): motion and sound for each, haptics on key events; success big, failure soft. No input lock during decorative motion; celebrations skippable; undo and hint wherever the top 3 have them. Pieces read at a glance: distinct silhouettes, an obvious selected state · grayscale and small-size captures. The same rules feel dull or alive by their layered feedback alone [F1].
- **Visuals and UI:** one art direction per the style guide; zero placeholders, grey boxes, default UI, missing glyphs or compression artifacts. The game's own UI skin (Portfolio rules). Motion as `oyun-hissi.md` §2. Text ≥11 pt on iOS and legible at web DPR 1 in 800×450; contrast 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for UI; targets ≥48 dp / 44 pt; safe areas, tablet, 16:9 web; screen scores as `arayuz-kalitesi.md` §5 [A5, G1, C2].
- **Audio and haptics:** a sound for every interaction; core-action SFX unique to this game; a seamless music loop; even levels, no clipping (ffmpeg `ebur128`). Separate music, SFX and haptics switches; haptics clear, sparse, in sync; the iOS silent switch respected; audio muted during ads.
- **Content and progression:** a 20-30 min first session and ≥7 days at median pace; a new element or twist at least every ~10 levels through level 50; the solver's curve has breathers and no wall before level 20 (`adil-zorluk-ve-basarisizlik.md`); a goal layer and a reason to return tomorrow (`hedef-katmanlari-ve-geri-donus.md`); a contact sheet of 12 random levels doesn't look like one level.
- **Ads and offers:** the first interstitial (level and minute), interstitials per 10 minutes, the rewarded offers and what each gives, what the player sees with no fill and offline · one table for all four: ours from a 15-minute run with test ads, the rivals' from the pre-research's play log (`on-arastirma.md` › Methods); `ilk-dakika.md`, `odullu-reklam.md`, `google-play-level-up.md` › Interstitial placement.
- **Performance:** mobile 60 fps (p95 frame ≤16.7 ms) in the heaviest level on the low-end target, 0 B GC a frame in play, memory under Play's thresholds, 0 crashes or ANRs in the gate's runs on the device matrix (`yayin-oncesi-test-listesi.md` › Device matrix; Play lowers visibility above a 1.09 % crash or 0.47 % ANR rate [G1]). Web: initial download ≤20 MB (the portals' cap is 50), ≤10 s to gameplay, Chrome, Edge and Safari, the same physics at 60 and 144 Hz, localStorage in try/catch, no external requests (`unity:optimize-web`) [C2, P1].
- **Accessibility:** every gameplay colour also carries a shape and passes a grayscale check (`renk-korlugu-palet.md`); reduce-motion and text-size options; English complete, Turkish an option [A5, F1].
- **Store and portal art:** the icon's main subject large: it reads at 48 px and stands out in a grid with the genre's top 10, not confusable by shape, colour or name (`oyun-simgesi.md`); screenshots 1-3 carry the hook in real gameplay in this game's own caption style; screenshot 1 shows the name, one promise and the genre's most crowded or most satisfying moment (leaders' first frames were full and bright where ours were sparse); captions readable at the store list's size (`store-listing-optimization.md` › Caption size); a 15-30 s preview video on the store page (the genre leaders: 3 of 9 to 6 of 10 by genre); the preview shows the hook in its first 3 s; the portal cover and thumbnails (still and animated) match the in-game art (`store-listing-optimization.md`). Storefront survey, Play US, four genres' top 10, 2026-10-23.
- **Differentiation:** the hook in the first 60 s and in screenshot 1; the top-3 table done; the portfolio checks green; the App Store dossier ready.

## Rubric
- Blind: every game of the set is scored in every area against the other three, so a reviewer needs no idea which is ours. 1 = broken or missing · 2 = below the weakest of the other three · 3 = on par with their median · 4 = equal to the best · 5 = better than all three, worth screenshot 1 (as Poki's Web Fit, where 3 = the category average [P2]).
- Differentiation: 1 = clone · 2 = reskin (a new theme only) · 3 = a minor twist · 4 = a clear twist visible in the first minute · 5 = a twist plus a theme, audience or meta combination none of the other three has.

| Area | Evidence (the same for A-D) | Min |
|---|---|---|
| First 60 s | cold-start video from the icon tap; time to input; taps to play | 4 |
| Core feel | 20 s clips of the same actions; latency table; feedback matrix | 3 |
| Art and animation | 6 captures and 3 clips of the same moments | 3 |
| UI and readability | home, HUD, win, lose, shop and settings; measurements | 3 |
| Audio and haptics | clips with sound; haptics list | 3 |
| Content and progression | level count, twist timeline, difficulty curve, contact sheet | 3 |
| Ads and offers | the ads-and-offers table: first interstitial, per 10 minutes, rewarded offers, no fill and offline | 3 |
| Technical | fps, memory, load, size and crash table | 3 |
| Accessibility | colour-blind simulations; settings list | 3 |
| Store and portal art | icon in the genre grid; screenshots 1-3, also at the list's size; the store video (present? its first 5 s) | 3 |
| Differentiation | `ozellikler.md`; the captures | 4 |

- **Area score:** the mean of the two reviewers; a third reviewer when they differ by ≥2 on one of our areas, then the median of the three. 4.5 is not a 5.
- **Pass:** every area at its minimum and a mean ≥3.5; at least one area at 5, and the hook's area (named in the dossier before scoring) is one of them; every hard gate green: 0 crashes or ANRs in the device matrix, an Android game draws again after the system's full-screen notice (`hakem-odasi.py first-launch`, Tools), 60 fps on the low-end target, the web size and load caps (≤20 MB, the portals' 50 MB only with a written reason; ≤10 s), no placeholder, English complete.
- **Blocks the release:** a red hard gate; an area under its minimum; a mean under 3.5; no 5, or the 5 isn't the hook; the hook missing from the first 60 s or screenshots 1-2; a portfolio flag; the fresh player stuck in levels 1-3 or on a screen (until fixed and passed by a new fresh player); missing evidence.

## Who scores
1. The builder captures and measures, never scores. The area's expert (`unity-uzmani` in Unity games) may check the measurements, never the taste.
2. Two fresh reviewers in a judge room (`hakem-odasi.py`, Tools), given only `kor/` and the reviewer form of `sablonlar/kalite-puan.md` (no project, key or dossier), score every letter in every area with one line of reason a score; the tool saves each answer as `hakem-<n>.md`, the main session merges ours into `puan.md` through the key. Not reviewer subagents: a subagent sees the main session's working folder, so the game's name, and a general-purpose one loads the project's CLAUDE.md [2026-10-24]. New reviewers each loop. The same room for every blind judgment of ours against others (a video's fresh scorer, a blind second eye).
3. A fresh player: a session that has never seen the project plays the gate build for 10 minutes from a clean install, one player profile, no hints: a web build in a browser it drives; on Android, `hakem-odasi.py play` (Tools), a headless session in an unnamed room whose only tools are open_game, screenshot, tap, swipe, back and wait (no files, commands or package name; its actions and frames saved beside the answer). It answers four questions (keep playing? the one thing? what felt cheap? which rival would you pick, and why? the last after looking at `kor/`'s captures) and logs where it got stuck, for how long and what it tried. It judges clarity, not fun: stuck in levels 1-3 or on a screen → a fix, and the gate passes only when a new fresh player gets through; its other answers go beside the scores to the reviewer of record; the answers are saved as `oyuncu-<n>.md` (`sablonlar/kalite-puan.md` › Taze oyuncunun oturumu). Short reactions: feedback under a second (a shake, a ring) falls between screenshots taken seconds after a tap, and a player wrote "nothing happened" [2026-10-23]: the judge room's player is told to write "I saw no reaction" instead; a web build's player looks at the three frames of the first second after a tap (or a short recording); the reviewer of record checks such a line against a recording before it counts as stuck. One player a round (~15 min). It replaced the owner's 10 minutes (the owner, 2026-10-22: no tests by him).
4. Optional real players for web builds (check Poki's exclusivity terms first): a Poki Playtest (10 videos of real players with inputs and console, often within minutes) and Player Fit (500 players in ~5 h; passes at an average above 3 min with ≥25 % of plays over 3 min; successful games 5+ min) [P2]. CrazyGames Basic Launch is a submission, so only after `GEÇTİ`; it runs 7-21 days (ends at ≥7 days live and ≥500 plays), successful titles reach 10+ min average playtime, top titles turn 80 %+ of players into ≥1 min of play, load in <10 s and stay under 20 MB, a strong D1 is 10-15 % [C3]: its numbers feed the next version (BAKIM.md › After release).
5. The reviewer of record (the session manager where a machine-level CLAUDE.md names one, else the owner) writes the result as the first line of `puan.md`: `KAPI: GEÇTİ <version>` or `KAPI: KALDI <version>`. The comprehensive test stage starts only after `GEÇTİ`.

## Loops
- After the first scoring, at most 2 loops: fix the lowest areas in this order: blockers, first minute, core feel, visuals and UI, audio, store art; then re-capture, re-score (new reviewers) and a new fresh player.
- Still failing after loop 2 → the reviewer of record decides: rework (a plan of its own), ship only where it fits (e.g. Android or web only), or shelve. No day budget: agents work continuously.
- Publishers gate the same way: Voodoo weighs product metrics with marketability instead of hard cut-offs, SayGames' producers play every game before a test, Supercell killed 30+ games against 5 hits [E1].

## Portfolio rules (against the studio's own games)
- **Which games:** "the studio's other games" are its apps released or to be released on a store or portal, shelved ones included (they stay live on the same accounts). A shelved game whose folder is gone joins the check through `git clone --depth 1` into a temporary folder (the agent's working folder, never a project folder), deleted when the check ends. Video projects (channels that render scenes) aren't apps: never in the comparison (Apple's 4.3 and Play's repetitive-content rule judge apps; a pack shared with a video counts nothing).
- **A per-game UI skin:** a shared UI kit (`layerlab-gui-pro.md`) is structure only; frames, palette, fonts, icons and home layout differ from the studio's other games · a side-by-side sheet of all studio games' home, HUD and win screens in the evidence folder.
- **Per-game store art:** captions, frames and colours of the store screenshots come from this game's own world, never a caption band or layout shared with other studio games (five games' screenshots 1-3 once shared one script's band) [2026-10-23]; beside another studio game's screenshots 1-3 it is told apart at a glance · the same sheet with screenshots 1-3. Also in the differentiation checks after a 4.3(a) answer.
- **Core-action SFX unique per game.**
- **0 shared gameplay art or audio files** with other studio games, by hash · `varlik-cakisma.py`. Asset packs are a fine source, but the same pack file in two studio games counts: take other parts, give them this game's own textures or palette, or allow scenery that carries no look with a reason. Typical deliberate parts for the allow list: the studio logo, cross-promotion images of the other games.
- **Listing texts** (description, promotional text, what's new) ≤25 % similar to any other studio listing · `metin-benzerlik.py`. A heuristic: Apple publishes no number; one developer's batch had release notes 46 % alike, cut to 15 % [A4].
- **No purchased complete-project templates** (4.2.6, 4.3(a)); asset packs are fine.
- Why: Apple names the same code or assets and look-alikes from one template [A3]; CrazyGames refuses games easily confused by name or iconography [C2]; Poki refuses asset flips [P1]; Google Play's Repetitive Content rule refuses near-identical apps [G1].

## Store and portal submissions
- **App Store, one at a time:** one new game in App Review per developer account; the next only after approval, 1-2 weeks apart (a heuristic); never a same-day batch through the API [A2, A4].
- **Review Notes** of every submission carry the dossier (`sablonlar/farklilik-dosyasi.md` › App Store): the hook sentence; 3 named rivals with 3 concrete differences each (mechanic, content, presentation); what is ours and what is licensed: designed and built in-house (our code, game design, levels and UI look), the licensed asset packs named from the game's licence list `tasarim/lisanslar.md` (e.g. 3D models, a UI kit, sound packs), the in-house libraries our games share, no app template, purchased source code or reskin; the engine and SDK list; where the hook shows ("0:20 of the attached video, level 2"); plus the version's usual notes (`app-store-game-center.md` §7). Never "all code, art and audio were made for this game" where a pack is used: a false line weakens a 4.3 answer. Attachment: a 30-60 s recording from launch to the hook and 2 annotated screenshots. Facts only (`metin-denetimi.md`).
- **After a 4.3:** first ask in Resolution Center which finding applies: binary, metadata or concept; never resubmit the same build with an argument; the one appeal only with new evidence; a precise question can go to Meet with App Review [A2, A4].
- **Consolidation** (several games in one app) is Apple's own suggested route for look-alike apps [A2], but the owner's call; the template default is separate apps, each passing the gate.
- **Portals:** resubmit a rejected game only after `GEÇTİ`, with the list of changes; CrazyGames asks for meaningful improvements and may restrict repeat weak submitters [C1-C2]. GameDistribution wants its SDK and ≥3 thumbnails (512×512 and 512×384) and reviews for up to 3 weeks [D1].

## Tools
- `metin-benzerlik.py <this game's listing files> -- <other games' listing files, folders or globs>`: the highest pairwise similarity, as the difflib ratio on the word sequences and the word 5-gram Jaccard; exit 1 above 0.25 (`--threshold`). Studio listings measured 2026-10-22: distinct games ≤0.13 and 0.00; a web copy of the same game 1.00. Run it a second time against the top 3's store texts (saved from their pages): a listing close to a rival's reads as a copy too.
- `varlik-cakisma.py <this project> -- <other projects or a quoted glob>`: sha256 of the audio, image and model files under `Assets/` (no `*.meta`, SDK or editor folders), shared files by folder; `--allow <file>`: a path glob or a sha256, then `# reason` (keep it at `tasarim/kalite-kapisi/izinli-ortak.txt`); exit 1 on shared gameplay files. The other projects: the studio's apps only, shelved clones included (Portfolio rules › Which games); a video project in the list gave 46 false shared files with a game (a 3D pack used in both, 2026-10-23). 2026-10-22: one game against the 21 other studio projects in 13 s (45,554 files), 39 shared: UI kit sprites, pack models, UI sounds, cross-promotion images.
- The first two read only, standard library, `--dene` runs a self-test in memory.
- `hakem-odasi.py run --evidence <kor/> --form <the reviewer form> --out <the version folder> --forbid "<store name>"`: copies the evidence into an unnamed temporary folder and runs each reviewer there as its own headless Explore session (`claude -p --agent Explore`: no CLAUDE.md, memory, hooks or MCP; Read, Glob and Grep inside the room only), the prompt passed verbatim; each answer to `hakem-<n>.md` as soon as its reviewer is done, a log (files each reviewer opened, denied access, the prompt) to `hakem-odasi-log.md`. `--out` is a project folder: a session's scratchpad (Claude Code's temp tree) is refused, since an empty folder there vanished during a run and two answers were lost [2026-10-24]; a folder that vanishes anyway is created again, and an answer that can't be written goes to a backup folder and to stdout. It stops (exit 3) when the game's name (the repo's folder and remote, `--forbid`) is in a file name, a text or a file's metadata, or a key file is in the evidence; it strips PNG and JPEG metadata and leaves out video and audio (a reviewer can't open them: give frames or a contact sheet). It can't read text inside images and the account's e-mail stays in the reviewer's context: mask names and logos. `--dry-run` shows the prompt without running a reviewer. Reviewers run at effort xhigh with a 5-minute prompt cache; `--effort max` only in the last decision round before a store [2026-10-24: on two real rounds xhigh found what max found at ~0.57 of its cost and half its time; one of two high reviewers missed a rewind max caught]. Over 100 images it warns: big rooms ran past the 30-minute timeout at max, nothing kept; give one contact sheet per clip.
- `hakem-odasi.py play --package <android package> --form <the questions> --out <the version folder> [--evidence <kor/>] [--apk <the installed file>]`: the fresh player on the emulator. The tool's own MCP server calls adb with fixed arguments only (start the game, screencap, input tap, swipe, back), scales screenshots and maps taps to device pixels, keeps the clock (`--minutes`, 10) and saves `oyuncu-<n>.md`, `oyuncu-<n>-actions.md` (every action with m:ss) and contact sheets of what the player saw (`oyuncu-<n>-frames-1.jpg` …, 40 numbered frames a sheet; the raw frames are deleted once the answer is written, `--keep-frames` keeps them) [2026-10-24: raw session frames were never deleted and filled the disk]. Before the player starts it prints the installed build (versionName, versionCode, install time); `--apk` stops the run unless the device's APK has that file's sha256 [2026-10-24: a round played the morning's build, the new install had failed silently]. The player can't leave the game [2026-10-24: back led a player into the Play Store's account and purchase screens]: the Play Store is stopped first, open_game waits until the game is in front, no screenshot, tap, swipe or back happens while anything else is in front (the game is brought back; another app left on the game's task, such as a sign-in or a search the game opened, is closed with back, else stopped), and back is refused on a screen where it closed the game. Android's one-time full-screen notice ("Viewing full screen") is confirmed on the device first and cleared if it shows anyway [2026-10-24: it covered a game for a whole round]; to see it again on a device (the test list's first-launch row): `adb shell settings delete secure immersive_mode_confirmations`. A device that has just booted is given a minute first. The form holds only the questions. The device has no Google account (an account's avatar names the studio). On a new device, first a one-minute run with `--package com.android.settings`.
- `hakem-odasi.py first-launch --package <android package> [--apk <the installed file>]`: model-free; `--apk` as in `play` (the fixed build tried must be the one installed). The fresh player never meets Android's full-screen notice (it's confirmed first), so this checks it separately: a control launch, where the game's own window must come to the front and its game area (10-92 % of the height) be drawn, not black and not one flat colour, in two frames in a row; its settled frame is kept. Then the notice is brought back (the secure setting deleted) and closed with "Got it" (tapped again, then confirmed in the settings, if it stays); once it has gone the game must draw what it drew without it within 15 s (`--seconds`). The setting goes back as it was. Exit 0 passed (also when no notice comes: the game doesn't hide the system bars), 1 black or one flat colour, 3 inconclusive (another window in front, a native window with text over the game such as Play's dialog on a sideloaded Play build or a consent form (Unity draws its own UI without text nodes), black without the notice too, the notice not closing, or another screen: look at `first-launch-control.jpg` and `first-launch-notice.jpg`). A fixed build is tried with `--apk`, and in the same session a known-black build first (the positive control), when there is one. A hard gate (Rubric › Pass) [2026-10-24: after the notice a studio game stayed black whichever way it was closed: Unity 6000.3's U28; `yayin-oncesi-test-listesi.md` › First launch full screen. 23:0x: the first version passed on the first frame that wasn't black; the notice's own box, a Play Store page brought to the front and a blank window passed the very build whose black screen had been seen, on two emulators].
