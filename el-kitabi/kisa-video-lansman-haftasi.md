# Short-video launch week (no ad budget)

## Summary
- Each clip is tested on its own: a small audience first, then wider if people watch instead of swiping; the first seconds decide. No advantage from more posts a day or a particular hour (YouTube).
- Store links are clickable only in some places on each platform; set up the path from clip to store first.
- Free tools only; anything paid is the session manager's call. Store-side promotion: `store-events.md` (Play promotional content, App Store In-App Events), `store-listing-optimization.md` (the listing).

## Clickable paths to the store
- **YouTube:** URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments (pinned ones too) are not clickable. Clickable: a Short's "related video" (needs the channel's advanced features: phone verification, then channel history or ID), up to 14 links on the channel profile, long-form video descriptions.
  - **Per game, one landscape "how to download" video** (30-60 s of gameplay, then how to get it; store links in its description). Every Short of that game links it as its related video. Whether the Data API can set the related video: not checked; else in YouTube Studio.
- **TikTok:** a verified business account adds a link or an app-download link to videos and the profile. Any account size can apply; Türkiye is a supported market (features vary by market); 18+, a business document, 1-5 business days of review → apply ≥7 days before launch. The owner decides. Bio link for personal accounts at 1,000 followers: third-party claim, unverified.
- **Instagram:** the Story link sticker (missing on brand-new accounts) and up to 5 bio links. Clickable caption links exist only in the paid Meta Verified test: no.

## Clips and originality
- Teasers are standalone gameplay hooks: the promise by 3 s, the hook by 6 s (TikTok's creative guidance), e.g. a solve or a near miss in the first 1-3 s (a last-moment fit; near-miss clips are common in puzzle ads, but that data comes from paid ads, not organic). The date is a corner label plus an end card; no countdown videos; not over-polished.
- One clean master per clip, no other app's watermark; upload natively to each platform. The same clip on three different platforms, even on the same day, is fine.
- **Never the same clip twice on the same platform**, and never delete and re-post. A re-cut changes the first 2 s and the level; Turkish and English cuts use different levels (near-identical cuts may count as repeats: our inference).
  - Instagram recommends only the first upload of identical content, and not videos with a visible watermark (2024); extended to photos and carousels in April 2026 (Tubefilter).
  - YouTube: since 2026-10-01 re-uploads of other people's videos without added value lose reach (PPC Land; our own clips aren't the target); deleting and re-uploading can count as spam (Creator Insider).
  - TikTok: "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" is ineligible for For You (Community Guidelines › Integrity and Authenticity); a corrected re-upload must differ (new hook, text or end card). Another platform's watermark keeps a video out of For You (third-party summary).

## TikTok For You eligibility (Community Guidelines, effective 2026-09-24)
- A separate standard from removal: an ineligible video stays up and is found by search and the profile, but isn't recommended; TikTok Studio's analytics flag it. A Promote rejection "not eligible for the For You feed" comes from this flag, not from the ad rules (a promoted video counts as an ad while it runs; only public videos).
- Ineligible: content pushing viewers to send gifts or inflate likes or follows → no "like/follow" calls in captions, hooks or end cards. A comment question isn't named in the rules: keep it, judge by results.
- Commercial content needs the content disclosure setting ("Your brand"); undisclosed, it can lose visibility or TikTok switches the setting on itself.
- The AI-generated label is required only for realistic AI-made or heavily edited scenes or people; game footage doesn't need it.
- "Automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems" are prohibited (removal, restriction, ban). Post one video at a time at a human pace (≥1 h apart, 2-3 a day), never in bulk; any warning or restriction on the account → stop browser automation there and tell the owner.
- No For You line on game violence in "Sensitive and Mature Themes" (checked 2026-10-15); still start a new account's tests with non-violent games. An appeal path for a flagged video: not found on an official page.
- Rules change often: recheck every 6 months.

## Timeline
- **L-5…L-1:** YouTube advanced features on, profile links set, the how-to-download video up; TikTok business verification applied for; Instagram link sticker checked. Pick the launch hook with Instagram Trial Reels (phone only, not on the web; shown to non-followers first; metrics after ~24 h; can go to followers if it does well within 72 h): 2-3 variants, the lowest skip rate and the most sends per reach win.
- **Store, free:** Play pre-registration only when the store declarations are ready (Google suggests starting 3-6 weeks ahead; launch notification and auto-install on eligible devices; up to 90 days; at most 2 titles at once; no pre-registration reward: an undelivered one can get the app suspended). iOS pre-order 2-180 days ahead (App Review) until the iOS version ships. In a 5-day window both bring few sign-ups (a vendor's view: pre-registration grows existing demand, it doesn't create it); the gain is a working store link before launch.
- **L0:** on a first Play release L0 is the approval day, known only when it comes (no managed publishing: `google-play-level-up.md` › Direct release) → keep the L0 clips uploaded privately and go public the hour Play approves. The "now on Google Play" clip on all three platforms within the same hour, pinned on TikTok; every Short's related video is the game's long video. After the release: one custom store listing URL per platform (`&listing=<name>`; up to 50, only after release) for TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, also in the profiles.
- **L+1…L+3:** a list of 20-30 micro-creators (puzzle, mahjong, cozy; Turkish and English; small and mid-size creators engage more than big ones, secondary source): the game link, 3 clean clips, an invitation to a YouTube Collaboration (up to 10 channels, Shorts too) or an Instagram collab post (its limit unverified). A fee asked → stop; the session manager decides.
- **After launch:** Play promotional content and Apple In-App Events (≤31 days, shown up to 14 days ahead) only for a real timed event in the game, never a "we launched" announcement; an event that isn't in the game, or is always there, gets less visibility. Store submissions need the session manager's approval.

## Measure (24 h and 72 h; a daily table L+1…L+7)
- YouTube: "How many chose to view" and engaged views (since 2025-03-31 every play counts as a view).
- Instagram: skip rate (first 3 s), the retention curve, sends per reach (sends weigh most for reaching non-followers).
- TikTok: average watch time, completion, profile visits, link clicks; at 48 h also Studio's For You eligibility flag next to the views.
- Play: store visitors → installs per custom listing (whether reports break it down by `&listing=`: unverified).
- Rule of thumb (ours, no source): a clip at ~2× the account's median views → 3-5 new cuts of that hook (new first 2 s, new level), never a repeat; below the median after 24-48 h → change only the hook. Shift the share toward the platform that brings the most store visits, not the total number of posts.

## Audience
- Calm games for older players (Lantern-type mahjong): weight YouTube and Facebook (older adults use them far more than TikTok or Instagram; Pew 2025), keep TikTok expectations low; slow pace, big tiles and text, Turkish titles. Reels can be shared to a Facebook page automatically (Accounts Center; menu unverified) once the page exists.
- Fast puzzle hooks: TikTok and Shorts first.
- Türkiye ad reach (DataReportal, Digital 2026): Instagram 62.3 M, YouTube 57.9 M, TikTok 44.9 M (18+), Facebook 34.7 M.

## Paid: don't
- Meta Verified (caption links), Google Ads pre-registration campaigns, TikTok Spark Ads and TikTok One creator campaigns, paid creator deals, auto-DM and paid link-in-bio tools.
- Business accounts have a limited music library (unverified): use the game's own music and sound.
